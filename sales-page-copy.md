## Services

### Car wash
Apni car ke liye apne area mein available wash service aur price dekhein, phir apni pasand ka time book karein.

### Bike wash
Bike wash bhi ghar par book karein. Service options aur price aapke service area ke hisaab se dikhte hain.

### Solar panel wash
Residential ya commercial solar panels ke liye quote request bhejein. Site inspection ke baad final quote share kiya jaata hai.

4. Booking aur service updates online check karein.

## Accuracy note

Home Wash is not available in the current beta. Vehicle prices depend on service area. Solar Wash requires inspection and a final quote before payment. Avoid promising fixed arrival times, universal coverage, or guaranteed results unless those details are confirmed for your live service area.
