Policies and support

English and Odia · Cuttack 42 Mouza beta

Service and booking terms

QuickWashHub currently accepts car, bike, and Solar Wash requests in the mapped 42 Mouza service area of Cuttack, Odisha. Availability is checked against the service-zone map.

Car and bike bookings show their service price before payment. Residential and commercial Solar Wash starts as a quote request. The team reviews the site details and inspection, then sends the final price for you to review. Payment is requested only after you accept that quote.

A booking is treated as paid only after Cashfree confirms payment to QuickWashHub. The assigned wash partner receives the exact pickup or site address after the job is assigned.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel an unpaid booking in your account. If payment has started or been completed, contact support with your booking reference. QuickWashHub will review the request and share the applicable cancellation or refund decision. Payment status and any refund are confirmed through the payment provider and your account.

Any special cancellation or refund condition shown during checkout for a particular service forms part of that booking.

Privacy and location

QuickWashHub uses account name and contact details, service address and location, vehicle or solar-site details, booking history, and service photos to arrange and support bookings. Device location is requested only when you choose the location feature. You can also select a saved address.

Exact service location is shared with the assigned partner and authorized operations staff for fulfilling the booking. Photos are stored privately and shared only with the customer, assigned partner, and authorized reviewers. Cashfree processes checkout and payment information; QuickWashHub stores payment references and status, not your card or UPI credentials.

You may request account deletion from the signed-in Support page. Some transaction and service records may need to be retained where applicable law requires it.

Grievance and customer support

Contact the grievance officer below or create a support ticket from your signed-in account. Include your booking ID or support reference where possible.
